# Choose your model and effort once, at the start

Your conversation is cached, and cache reads cost roughly a tenth of the normal
rate. That discount is the only reason a long session does not bankrupt you.

**The model is part of the cache key.** So is your effort level. Change either
one mid-session and none of your history matches the cache any more, so the whole
conversation gets reprocessed at full price.

Which means the decision in front of you is not *which model should I switch
to*. It is *which model am I starting on*. Pick the model and the effort level
before you begin, and then leave both alone until the session ends. If you want
to be on a cheaper model, start there — the saving is real when you take it at
the beginning and self-defeating when you take it in the middle.

This one has a trap attached to it that catches almost everybody, and it gets a
step of its own in the second half.
