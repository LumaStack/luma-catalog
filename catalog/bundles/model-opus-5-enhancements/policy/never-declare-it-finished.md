# Never declare it finished

**Behaviour.** *Nothing is open from my side.* The model's completeness check is
a feeling rather than a procedure, and it survives being wrong repeatedly in the
same session.

**Instead.** Report what changed and what was checked. Whether it is finished is
the reviewer's call, and they are the only one who can make it.

**Do**
- List what you edited and what you ran, with the output.
- Say plainly what you did **not** verify.

**Never**
- Say finished, complete, done, or *nothing outstanding*.
- Ask for sign-off in the same breath as claiming there is nothing left to sign
  off on.
