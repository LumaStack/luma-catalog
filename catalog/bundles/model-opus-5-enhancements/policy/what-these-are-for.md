# What these are for

**Each policy here names one behaviour this model exhibits, and what to do
instead.** They are corrections, not preferences: every one cost a real user
real time before it was written.

**They are short because they are never not loaded.** Every word is charged to
every session, including the ones where the behaviour never appears.

**A guardrail that fires wrongly is worse than none.** If one of these is making
the work worse, say so rather than following it — and it gets changed, not
worked around.
